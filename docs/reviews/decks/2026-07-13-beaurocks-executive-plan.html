Provider capability + quota posture
BEAUROCKS

A resilient catalog uses every source for the job it can legally and reliably perform.

YouTube

Embeddable stage media and karaoke backings. Reuse successful results for 72 hours, alias equivalent intents, verify playability, and index trusted playlists at low concurrency.

Apple Music

Host-owned library, background playlists, full-song fallback, and canonical metadata. Playback requires a connected MusicKit session.

Spotify

Discovery or external handoff only until playback, licensing, and capability policy support more. Never imply in-app playback that does not exist.

Uploads

Host-device background playback and content-agnostic fallback. “Playing” is reported only after the media element confirms it.

Reuse first.
The YouTube quota strategy

`search.list` is the expensive step. The compliant response is to reduce repeated live search, revalidate known IDs with lower-cost metadata calls, and request quota extension with evidence from real event demand—not scrape or bypass the API.

Large-event readiness: trusted backing reuse + canonical catalog + provider fallbacks + an evidence-backed YouTube quota-extension packet.

Points, BeauBucks + monetization
BEAUROCKS

Separate participation status from spendable event value.

Points
Reputation, participation, rankings, and recognition.
  • Earned through play and engagement
  • Not represented as cash value
  • Supports recurring identity across nights
BeauBucks
Premium or event-funded value used for deliberate room actions.
  • Can be included with paid admission
  • Can support performer-directed contribution intent
  • Server-priced, operation-ID protected, and auditable

Flexible policy, fixed clarity: a free-flowing house night and a $20 ticketed fundraiser can use the same primitives without making the currencies mean different things.

✓Canary spends are atomic, retry-safe, and paired with typed shadow ledger entries.
→Legacy room points remain balance authority until reconciliation and opening-balance gates are explicitly satisfied.
Economy principle // flexible policy, unambiguous value
